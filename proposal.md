# Capstone proposal

## Path
AfyaPlus Module 4 Capstone: integrated AI health-operations system (the official, recommended path).
Not the domain alternative.

## Problem
AfyaPlus clinic coordinators currently use separate tools from Weeks 6 to 10, each with its own login and rules: triage intake, stock look-ups and the listing checker.
One authenticated message door should send each request to the right tool. It should give triage advice grounded in clinic guidance and answer one clinic's stock question through the MCP tool. The Week 10 catalog lane stays separate.
The system supports one decision: what a coordinator does next (send to clinic, reorder, or review a listing). People decide and the system recommends.
We will know it worked when one command runs the whole flow with a printed transcript, the unit tests and security smoke pass in CI, and the golden-set eval scores at least 0.90.

## Dataset
fixtures/triage_messages_sample.jsonl: 300 synthetic, labelled intent rows (teaching only), generated by scripts/make_fixture.py.
services/catalog/fixtures/sokonihub_catalog.json: the 10 Week 10 SokoniHub listings, kept in their own folder.
services/rag/knowledge/: 7 teaching FAQ passages plus the 24-entry Week 5 knowledge.json.
Do not scrape production. Do not use millions of rows.
